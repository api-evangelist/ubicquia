## Glossary

- [Arc detection](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#arc-detection): Sensors and software that identify the optical, acoustic, or electrical signature of electrical arcing inside switchgear or transformers. Provides early warning before arc faults escalate into equipment damage or fires.
- [Distribution transformer monitoring](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#distribution-transformer-monitoring): Continuous, sensor-based measurement of voltage, current, temperature, and load on individual distribution transformers. Reveals overload, imbalance, and degradation conditions before they cause outages or asset failures.
- [Grid edge](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#grid-edge): The far end of a utility's distribution network — the transformers, secondary lines, and meters that connect to customer premises. Real-time visibility at the grid edge is what separates predictive maintenance from reactive truck rolls.
- [LED conversion](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#led-conversion): The replacement of legacy high-pressure sodium or mercury-vapor streetlights with LED fixtures. Reduces energy consumption significantly but creates new operational blind spots — LEDs report less about their own health unless paired with a smart-controller layer.
- [Power quality](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#power-quality): A measure of how closely the electrical supply matches the ideal — stable voltage, clean waveform, balanced phases. Poor power quality causes equipment damage, production downtime, and elevated energy bills for both utilities and their end customers.
- [SAIDI](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#saidi): System Average Interruption Duration Index — the average total time, in minutes per year, that customers in a utility's service territory go without power. Lower SAIDI indicates a more reliable distribution grid.
- [SAIFI](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#saifi): System Average Interruption Frequency Index — the average number of times a utility customer experiences a power outage in a year. Used alongside SAIDI to score grid reliability and inform regulatory performance reporting.
- [Situational awareness](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#situational-awareness): The continuous, real-time understanding of what is happening across an area or asset, combining video, sensor data, and analytics into a single view operators can act on. Foundational to modern public-safety and substation-protection deployments.
- [Smart streetlight](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#smart-streetlight): A streetlight enhanced with a sensor-equipped controller that reports its status, accepts remote dimming and switching commands, and acts as a pole-mounted platform for additional municipal sensors. The foundation for most smart-city deployments.
- [Substation monitoring](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#substation-monitoring): Real-time observation of substation equipment for thermal events, arcing, intrusion, and operational anomalies. Combines physical-security awareness with electrical telemetry to protect crews and infrastructure.
- [Thermal monitoring](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#thermal-monitoring): Continuous measurement of equipment temperature — typically via thermal imaging, infrared sensors, or thermocouples — to detect hot spots, overload, and insulation degradation in transformers, switchgear, and connections.
- [Truck roll](https://www.ubicquia.com/glossary#truck-roll): A field-service trip dispatched to diagnose or repair an asset. Each truck roll represents real cost in fuel, labor, and time; smart-infrastructure platforms are evaluated partly by how many truck rolls they eliminate.
